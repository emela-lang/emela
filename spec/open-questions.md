# 18. Open questions `[open]`

This is the list of points that are not yet agreed on. To decide one, write a proposal (`proposals/README.md`). A decided row is kept with strikethrough, followed by the decision and its date. Records of points that the draft and the implementation filled in tentatively are in `[history.filled]`.
